# Shipping Policy

Standard delivery normally takes 3 to 5 business days.

Express delivery normally takes 1 to 2 business days.

Customers receive tracking information when the order is shipped.

If an order is delayed:
- The customer can contact support.
- Support can investigate the shipment.
- The customer may request cancellation or a refund when eligible.

Orders marked as delivered but not received must be reported within 48 hours.
