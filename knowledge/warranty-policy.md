# Warranty Policy

Electronic products include a standard two-year warranty.

The warranty covers:
- Manufacturing defects.
- Hardware failures under normal use.
- Products that stop functioning during the warranty period.

The warranty does not cover:
- Accidental damage.
- Water damage.
- Unauthorized repairs.
- Damage caused by misuse.

Customers must provide their order identifier and a description of the issue.
The company may repair, replace, or refund an eligible product.
