# Refund Policy

Customers may request a refund within 30 calendar days of the order date.

Eligible orders:
- Delivered orders returned within 30 days.
- Delayed orders when the customer no longer wishes to wait.
- Cancelled orders that were already charged.
- Products received damaged or defective.

Non-eligible orders:
- Orders already refunded.
- Orders submitted more than 30 days ago.
- Products damaged by misuse.
- Items marked as non-returnable.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method after verification.
Processing usually takes 5 to 10 business days.
