# Customer Support FAQ

## How can I check an order?

Provide the exact order identifier, such as ORD-84721.

## How can I request a refund?

Provide the order identifier. Refund eligibility is determined by the company's refund rules.

## How long does a refund take?

Approved refunds usually take 5 to 10 business days.

## How can I use the warranty?

Provide the order identifier and explain the product issue.

## What should I do if my package is delayed?

Contact customer support with the order identifier so that the shipment can be investigated.
